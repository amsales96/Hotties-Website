I don't know anything about D&D. How do I learn?

Every month, we have beginner sessions, both online and in person. In a beginner session, we make you a character sheet and give a quick crash course on how to play the game. From there, we run a 2.5 hour game that gives you a feel for how D&D is played! If you come to an in-person session, you also get a free set of dice to take home.

What if I want to learn how to make a character?

We also have beginner-friendly character workshops, where our DMs teach you how building a character works and how to make your own! (It's easier if you attend a beginner session before this, so you understand the game!)
